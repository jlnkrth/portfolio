## Notes

Pain plus reflection equals progress. That formula alone was
worth the read. Every failed launch or botched hire is
tuition, but only if you sit down afterwards and write out
what the mistake actually was.

Treat your team like a machine you can debug. When something
breaks, don't just patch the outcome — fix the design of the
machine that produced it. That reframe changed how I run
retros.

Believability-weighted decisions beat flat democracy. The
person who has shipped the thing three times gets more
weight than the loudest voice in the room.

The work principles half drags. It's written for a
1,500-person hedge fund, not a five-person studio. Skim it,
keep the life principles close.
