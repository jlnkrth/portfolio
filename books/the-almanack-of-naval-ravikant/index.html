## Notes

Seek wealth, not money or status. Wealth is assets that
earn while you sleep. As someone who has billed a lot of
hours, that line stung — it's the whole argument for
building products and open source instead of only selling
time.

The leverage framework is the most useful model in the
book:

1. Labor and capital — old leverage, permissioned
2. Code and media — new leverage, permissionless
3. Zero marginal cost of replication is the unlock

Specific knowledge: the thing you can't be trained for,
that feels like play to you and looks like work to
everyone else. The design-engineering overlap is exactly
that for me — neither pure designers nor pure engineers
want to live there, so I do.

The happiness half is quieter but held up better than I
expected. Desire is a contract you make with yourself to
be unhappy until you get what you want. I reread that
chapter more than the wealth ones.
