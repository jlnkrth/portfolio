## Notes

Ries codified what good founders were already doing: build,
measure, learn — and do it fast. The MVP isn't a broken
product; it's the smallest experiment that tests your riskiest
assumption.

Validated learning beats vanity metrics. I still see teams
celebrating signups while retention flatlines. The book
gives you vocabulary to call that out without sounding
like a pessimist.

Pivot or persevere is a decision framework, not a vibe.
Set a threshold before you launch, then actually look at
the data when you hit it.

Some ideas aged poorly (innovation accounting can get
performative), but the core loop is still the best intro
to product experimentation I've found.
