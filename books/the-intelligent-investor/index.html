## Notes

Mr. Market is the single best mental model in finance: a
manic business partner who offers you a different price
every day. You're free to ignore him. Price is what the
crowd feels; value is what the business earns. Most of
investing is refusing to let the first thing dictate the
second.

Margin of safety is the other pillar — buy with enough
room that being wrong doesn't ruin you. I apply it beyond
stocks now: project buffers, runway estimates, timeline
promises to clients. Assume your analysis is off and size
accordingly.

Graham's split between investor and speculator was
clarifying. An investor analyzes, insists on safety of
principal, and accepts adequate returns. Everything else —
including most of what fintech apps gamify — is
speculation wearing a suit.

Real talk: the chapters on railroad bonds are a slog and
the numbers are ancient. Read chapters 8 and 20, plus
Zweig's commentary, and you have 90% of the value.
