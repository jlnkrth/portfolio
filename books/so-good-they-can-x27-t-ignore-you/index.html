## Notes

Follow your passion is terrible advice, and Newport
actually shows the work behind that claim. Passion is not
a preexisting thing you discover; it's a byproduct of
getting good at something rare and valuable.

Career capital is the frame I use with junior designers
now. Autonomy, mission, creative freedom — all the things
people want from work — are bought with skill. Demand them
before you've earned them and the market says no.

The craftsman mindset flips the question from what can
this job offer me to what can I offer this job. Ten years
into client work, the people I see plateau are the ones
still asking the first question.

His control traps are real. I hit the first one going
freelance too early — chasing autonomy without the capital
to back it up. The book would have saved me a rough year.
