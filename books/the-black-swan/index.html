## Notes

The Mediocristan vs. Extremistan split is the sharpest tool
in the book. Height lives in Mediocristan — no single
person changes the average. Wealth, book sales, and startup
outcomes live in Extremistan, where one outlier is the
whole story. Most bad forecasts come from using the wrong
map.

The turkey problem is the part I retell most: a thousand
days of being fed is overwhelming evidence the farmer loves
you, right up until Thanksgiving. Past stability is not
evidence against rupture — a lesson that applies to uptime,
clients, and careers.

The narrative fallacy hits close to home. We compress messy
randomness into clean stories after the fact, then mistake
the story for understanding. Every startup post-mortem I've
read does this.

Practical takeaway: stop trying to predict rare events and
build so they don't kill you. Barbell it — very safe on one
side, small aggressive bets on the other, nothing fragile
in the middle.
