## Notes

The golden circle — why, how, what — is simple to the
point of obvious, yet most companies genuinely communicate
from the outside in. Look at any SaaS landing page:
features first, purpose nowhere. With the startups I work
with, flipping that order is often the whole positioning
fix.

People don't buy what you do, they buy why you do it.
Overstated? Sure. But it explains why some brands get
evangelists and others get customers who churn at the
first price hike.

The law of diffusion section is the practical part: aim
early messaging at the innovators and early adopters who
buy on belief, not at the pragmatic majority who need
social proof you don't have yet.

Honest critique: it's a 20-minute TED talk stretched to
250 pages, and the Apple examples are cherry-picked. Read
it fast, keep the circle, move on.
