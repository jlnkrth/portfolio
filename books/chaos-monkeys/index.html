## Notes

The most cynical book about Silicon Valley I've read, and
probably the most honest. Martinez goes from Goldman to a
YC startup to selling ads at Facebook, burning every
bridge in print on the way out.

The adtech chapters are genuinely educational. If you want
to understand how targeting, auctions, and identity
matching actually work under the hood of the ad economy,
this beats most textbooks.

His line about investors, employees, and entrepreneurs is
the cleanest model of startup power dynamics I know. Once
you see who has money and who has time, every negotiation
suddenly makes sense.

Take the swagger with a grain of salt — he's the hero of
every anecdote — but the mechanics of acquihires, vesting,
and term sheets are real, and nobody else writes about
them this plainly.
