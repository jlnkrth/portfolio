## Notes

The one idea that carries the whole book: humans dominate
because we cooperate in huge numbers, and we do it through
shared fictions. Money, companies, nations, brands — none
of them exist outside our collective agreement that they
do.

That reframing is oddly practical for a designer. A brand
is a shared fiction under active construction. A startup is
a story trying to recruit enough believers — investors,
employees, users — to become self-sustaining. My job is
partly fiction engineering.

The agricultural revolution chapter is the most contrarian
bit: wheat domesticated us, trapping people in harder,
worse lives while the species thrived. A useful pattern —
technologies that win aren't necessarily the ones that make
individual lives better.

Harari overreaches in places and the second half thins out,
but as a lens for seeing institutions as stories, it
stuck.
