## Notes

The whole book is one rule wearing different togas: pay
yourself first. A tenth of everything you earn is yours to
keep, before rent, before anything. A century of personal
finance writing is basically footnotes to this.

What stuck from the parables:

1. Make your savings work — idle gold earns nothing
2. Guard principal — chasing returns you don't
   understand is how you lose it
3. Take advice only from people who practice the craft,
   not from the brickmaker on jewels
4. Increase your ability to earn — skill is the real
   compounding asset

As a freelancer-turned-studio-owner, the tenth-of-income
rule works better than any budgeting app I've tried. It's
dumb, it's automatic, and it survives bad months.

The fake old-timey prose grates after a while, but the
book is 124 pages. You can forgive a lot at that length.
