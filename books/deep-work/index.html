## Notes

The core claim: the ability to focus without distraction is
getting rarer at exactly the moment it's getting more
valuable. For anyone who builds things, that's the whole
business case for protecting your calendar.

Deep work is a skill, not a mood. You train it like a
muscle, and every glance at Slack detrains it. Attention
residue explains why a quick check between tasks quietly
costs me half an hour of real thinking.

Newport's four scheduling philosophies:

1. Monastic — disappear completely
2. Bimodal — long stretches on, long stretches off
3. Rhythmic — same deep block every day
4. Journalistic — switch on whenever you can

Rhythmic is the only one that survives client work. Deep
block in the morning, meetings pushed to the afternoon,
phone in another room.
