## Notes

This is a quote book — one Munger line per page with
Clark's commentary underneath. The commentary is thin, but
as a Munger sampler it earns its spot on the shelf.

The recurring theme is temperament over intellect. Munger
keeps saying the returns go to people who can sit still,
avoid envy, and not do anything dumb while everyone around
them is busy being clever.

Read back to back with Griffin's book, the overlap is big.
If you only read one, read Griffin. This one works better
as something you flip open at random before a hard call.

What I keep coming back to: envy is the dumbest sin
because it's the only one you can't have any fun at. That
reframe kills a lot of comparison anxiety in startup land.

Also a reminder that reading is the job. Munger claims he
has never known a wise person who didn't read constantly.
Neither have I.
