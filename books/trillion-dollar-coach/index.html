## Notes

Bill Campbell coached Steve Jobs, Larry Page, and Eric
Schmidt at the same time and never took a dollar for it.
This book is Google's attempt to write down his playbook
before it disappeared with him.

The core move: work the team, not the problem. When
something breaks, Campbell didn't debug the issue, he
debugged the people — who stopped talking to whom, whose
trust got dented. Fix that and the issue fixes itself.

Care personally, challenge directly. He'd hug you and then
tell you exactly what you were screwing up. The warmth is
what bought him the license to be blunt.

Only coach the coachable — honesty and humility are
prerequisites, not outcomes. That one sentence has saved
me from a couple of doomed engagements.

It's hagiography by design, written by people who loved
him. Extract the habits, skip the cult of Bill.
