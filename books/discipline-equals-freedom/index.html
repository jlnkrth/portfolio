## Notes

Short, blunt, and mostly right. Willink's core argument is
that discipline isn't the opposite of freedom — it's the
price of admission. Wake up early, train, do the hard thing
first, and the rest of the day gets easier.

The "default aggressive" mindset maps onto shipping: don't
wait for perfect conditions, don't over-debate, execute
with what you have and adjust in contact.

It's repetitive if you've read Extreme Ownership. Treat this
as the pocket edition — good for rereading on a bad morning
when you're negotiating with the snooze button.

The stoic framing without the philosophy lectures is the
appeal. Less Ryan Holiday, more drill instructor.
