## Notes

Naming the enemy is half the book's value. Resistance is
whatever stops you from sitting down and doing the work —
and it shows up as procrastination, research, perfectionism,
even busywork that looks productive. I recognize all of
mine now.

The fear compass is the sharpest idea: the more a project
scares you, the more it matters. Resistance is strongest
exactly where the work is most important. I check this
before dropping a side project — is it boring, or is it
scary?

Turning pro is a decision, not a milestone. The pro shows
up every day, works whether inspired or not, and doesn't
take failure or success personally. Amateurs wait to feel
ready.

The muse mysticism in part three isn't for me, but it's
short. The first hundred pages are the ones I reread every
time a project stalls.
