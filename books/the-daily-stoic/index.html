## Notes

One page per day, one idea per page. It's a delivery system
for Marcus Aurelius and Seneca without requiring you to
parse 2,000-year-old sentence structure before coffee.

The recurring theme: control what you can, release what you
can't, and stop adding drama to events that are already
neutral. That maps cleanly onto client work, launches, and
anything involving other humans.

I don't read it daily anymore, but I keep it on the desk for
weeks when everything feels reactive. The format lowers the
activation energy.

Holiday repackages ancient philosophy for modern
professionals. Some purists roll their eyes; I care whether
it helps me show up better on Tuesday.
