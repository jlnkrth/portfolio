## Notes

Advertising is salesmanship, not art. Ogilvy hammers this
on nearly every page, and it maps straight onto design
work: a landing page that wins awards but doesn't convert
is a failure, full stop.

The headline math changed how I budget my effort. Five
times as many people read the headline as the body, so the
headline deserves five times the iterations. Same logic
applies to hero sections and app store screenshots.

His rules that still hold in 2026:

1. Do the homework — research before creative
2. One big idea, or the ad sails past unnoticed
3. Long copy sells, if every line earns its keep
4. Respect the reader's intelligence

Some chapters are pure 1980s Madison Avenue time capsule.
Read it for the principles, not the tactics.
