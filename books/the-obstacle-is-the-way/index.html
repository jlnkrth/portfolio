## Notes

Stoicism packaged for people who won't read Marcus
Aurelius directly — which, honestly, was me. The frame is
three disciplines: perception, action, will. See things
plainly, act on what you control, endure what you can't.

The core move — the impediment to action advances action —
maps cleanly onto engineering work. A blocked API, a
budget cut, a dead-end design: each one narrows the
solution space, and the narrower space is usually where
the better idea was hiding.

Turning dead time into alive time changed how I handle
waiting — on client approvals, on deploys, on hiring. The
gap is where the prep happens, not where progress stops.

Style-wise it's anecdote, lesson, repeat, and some of the
history gets smoothed over to fit the thesis. But as a
pocket reference for bad weeks it earns its shelf space.
