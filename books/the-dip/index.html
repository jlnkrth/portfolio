## Notes

Quitting isn't the opposite of grit — it's how you fund
it. Godin's argument: strategic quitters dump dead ends
fast so they have the resources to push through the one
dip that's actually worth crossing.

The three curves are a useful diagnostic for any project
on my plate:

1. The Dip — hard middle, big payoff if you push through
2. The Cul-de-Sac — flat forever, quit now
3. The Cliff — feels fine until it suddenly doesn't

The rule I took away: decide your quitting conditions
before you start, in writing. Quitting in the middle of
the dip is the worst outcome — you pay the cost and skip
the reward.

Being best in the world sounds grandiose until you notice
the world part is up to you. Best design studio for
early-stage fintech in your region is a winnable market.
Best design studio is not.

It's a 96-page blog post, honestly. But I've reread it
before every quit-or-double-down decision since.
