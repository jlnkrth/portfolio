## Notes

Talent density is the whole trick. Every other Netflix
policy — no vacation limits, no expense approvals, no
KPIs — only works because they pay top of market and cut
adequate performers. Copy the freedom without the density
and you get chaos.

Lead with context, not control. This is the line I quote
most to founders. If you have to approve every decision,
you either hired wrong or you're hoarding information your
team needs to decide well.

The keeper test is uncomfortable and useful: if this
person quit tomorrow, would I fight to keep them? Asking
it honestly about a team surfaces problems everyone
already knows about but nobody names.

The candor chapters travel less well than the book admits.
Erin Meyer's culture-mapping sections are the honest part:
what reads as directness in Amsterdam reads as an insult
in Tokyo.
