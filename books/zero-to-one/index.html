## Notes

Competition is for losers. The goal is not to beat rivals at
their own game but to find a secret — something true that few
people agree with you on — and build a monopoly around it.

Thiel's four points stuck with me:

1. Proprietary technology — 10x better, not 10% better
2. Network effects — start small, dominate, then expand
3. Economies of scale — software wins here by default
4. Branding — real brands come from substance, not ads

The chapter on definite vs. indefinite optimism reframed how I
think about planning. A definite optimist has a plan and builds
toward it. An indefinite one keeps options open and ends up
owning nothing.
