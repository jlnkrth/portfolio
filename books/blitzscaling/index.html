## Notes

Blitzscaling means prioritizing speed over efficiency in
the face of uncertainty — deliberately doing inefficient
things because being first to scale matters more than
being right about the details. It's the opposite of almost
every other book on this shelf, which is exactly why it's
worth reading.

The growth-factor framing is the practical part: market
size, distribution, gross margins, and network effects on
one side; product/market fit and operational scalability
as the limiters. I run it as a checklist whenever a client
says "we want to scale."

Uncomfortable but true: at each stage — family, tribe,
village, city, nation — the company has to fire its old
processes. What got you to 10 people actively hurts at
100.

The caveat the fans skip: blitzscaling is for
winner-take-most markets with capital to burn. Most
companies I work with should not blitzscale, and to its
credit the book says so plainly.
