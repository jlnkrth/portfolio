## Notes

Most of the lifestyle-design framing hasn't aged well, but
the operating principles underneath are still sharp. I read
this less as "escape your job" and more as "stop doing work
that doesn't matter."

The DEAL framework in practice:

1. Definition — decide what you actually want; fear-setting
   beats goal-setting
2. Elimination — 80/20 everything and go on a
   low-information diet
3. Automation — build systems and delegate outcomes, not
   tasks
4. Liberation — decouple income from location and hours

Parkinson's Law is the one I apply weekly: work expands to
fill the time you give it. Tight, artificial deadlines
produce better output than generous ones.

Being busy is not the same as being productive. Ferriss is
right that busyness is usually a way to avoid the few
uncomfortable actions that actually matter.
