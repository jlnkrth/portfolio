## Notes

The Rider-Elephant-Path model is the most useful framing of
change I've come across. The Rider is the rational planner,
the Elephant is emotion and habit, and the Path is the
environment they move through.

The playbook that stuck:

1. Direct the Rider — follow the bright spots and script
   the critical moves
2. Motivate the Elephant — shrink the change until starting
   feels easy
3. Shape the Path — tweak the environment so the right
   thing is the default

I use this constantly in product work. When users resist a
new flow, it's almost never a people problem. Clarify the
next action, make the first step tiny, or change the
interface — don't write another announcement post.
