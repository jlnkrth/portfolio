## Notes

Grant's best point: originals aren't reckless risk-takers.
They feel fear and doubt like everyone else — they just
manage it differently. Procrastination can be productive
if it gives ideas time to incubate.

"Vuja de" — seeing something familiar with fresh eyes — is
a design superpower. The best product critiques come from
people who haven't normalized the broken flow yet.

The chapter on speaking up against bad ideas without getting
fired is practical org politics. Useful when you're the
person who sees the cliff the team is walking toward.

It's pop psychology with studies attached. Cherry-pick the
ideas that match your situation; don't treat every finding
as universal law.
