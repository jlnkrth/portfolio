## Notes

Voss's premise: negotiation is emotional, not rational, so
stop bargaining like an economist. Tactical empathy — make
the other side feel understood before you steer — beats
splitting the difference every time.

The tools I actually use on client calls:

1. Mirroring — repeat their last three words, then wait
2. Labeling — "it seems like budget is the real worry"
3. Calibrated questions — "how am I supposed to do that?"
4. The late-night FM DJ voice when things get tense

Getting to "no" was the reframe for me. People feel safe
saying no, so ask questions where no is progress: "is now
a bad time?" opens more doors than chasing a hollow yes.

Yes is nothing without how. I stopped celebrating verbal
agreement and started asking implementation questions
until the deal was real. Fewer ghosted proposals since.
