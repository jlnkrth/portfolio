## Notes

Incentives explain almost everything, and they're usually
not the incentives on the org chart. The sumo wrestlers
and the cheating teachers both respond perfectly to the
system they're actually in, not the one on paper. Every
product metric I've ever set has had a sumo problem
hiding in it somewhere.

Information asymmetry is the other big hammer. Real estate
agents leave their own houses on the market longer than
their clients' — they capture the upside of waiting, you
don't. Whenever an expert's advice and their commission
point the same way, be suspicious.

Correlation vs. causation is the chapter I wish every
stakeholder had read. More police and less crime can both
be caused by a third thing. Dashboards make this mistake
at scale every day.

The abortion-crime chapter is the famous one and the
shakiest — the methods took real criticism afterwards.
Good reminder that clever beats careful only until
someone checks the math.
