About

Julian Kreth — Building websites for startups across the globe.

Where I’m from

I was born and raised in Germany and have lived in Hamburg since 2021. Before that, I bounced around a bit — but always within reach of good bread and bad weather. My parents weren’t particularly technical, but they supported my curiosity early on. I spent most of my time online, building things, breaking them, and occasionally forgetting to go outside.

What I used to do

I’ve worn a lot of hats: ran my own agency, built client funnels, pitched investors, managed chaos. Before that, I was freelancing, juggling way too many projects at once, trying to figure out if I was a designer who codes or a developer who cares about design. Turns out it’s both.

What I do now

These days I work as a tech lead herding AI agents while maintaining the company’s Claude plugin — mostly remote, mostly US-based clients. I help startups ship clean, scalable websites, and I still write custom code when the platform says no. I’m also building Commentblocks — a lightweight feedback tool for Webflow freelancers and agencies.

Where I’m at now

Hamburg, sixth floor, overlooking a noisy road. Not exactly Yosemite, but I hike when I can, take long baths when I can’t, and occasionally go off-grid to remember what quiet feels like.

What I’m looking for

Projects that don’t just check boxes. People who value clarity over fluff. And a little room to build the kind of tools I wish I had when I started.
